💳 Payment Policy

To confirm an order, customers are required to pay 9% of the total cost in advance.

The remaining 91% is paid upon delivery of the products.

🔁 Return Policy

We accept returns under the following conditions:

  • Items must be returned within 48 hours after delivery
  • Products must be unused and in original condition
  • Damaged or incorrect items qualify for replacement or refund

Return shipping costs may apply depending on the reason for return.

To request a return, please contact us via WhatsApp or through our contact page.
